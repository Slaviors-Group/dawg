# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DAWG ("we," "us," or "our") handles data when you use our browser extension, desktop application, and CLI tools. Because DAWG is a local-first platform designed for capturing and reproducing web application glitches, our data collection and processing methods are fundamentally designed to keep you in control of your data.

## 1. Local-First Architecture (Zero Data Exfiltration)

DAWG operates strictly as a **local-first** application. All artifacts generated by the DAWG capture process are stored entirely and exclusively on your local machine.

We **never** automatically upload, transmit, or synchronize your data, bug reports, or artifacts to our servers or any third-party services. Any sharing of DAWG artifacts (e.g., uploading to a registry, emailing to a colleague, or attaching to a bug tracker) is a manual action performed solely at your discretion.

## 2. What Data DAWG Captures (The "Artifact")

When you initiate a capture sequence, the DAWG browser extension and local engine intercept and record high-fidelity telemetry to create a reproducible artifact. This artifact (`application/vnd.dawg.*`) may contain the following data:

*   **Application & Environment State:**
    *   Operating System details, application versions, and a frozen clock state.
    *   Database fixtures representing the exact state of your application's database at the time of capture.
*   **Network Activity:**
    *   HTTP requests and responses (cassettes) intercepted between the browser and the backend server. These may contain headers, session tokens, cookies, and payload data.
*   **User Interactions (DOM Snapshots):**
    *   DOM mutations, mouse movements, and UI interactions recorded via `rrweb`. 
    *   **Input Masking:** By default, all form inputs (passwords, text fields, etc.) are masked (`maskAllInputs: true`) in the visual DOM snapshot stream. However, explicit input actions (clicks and text fills) are captured separately as replayable events to ensure determinism.
*   **Session Metadata:**
    *   Repository commit hashes (`repoCommit`).
    *   Reporter identifiers and environmental tags.
    *   Timestamps and randomized seeds used to ensure deterministic replay.

## 3. How Data is Processed & Sanitized

To prevent accidental data leaks (such as capturing production Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or sensitive credentials), the DAWG local engine employs a **Deny-by-Default** sanitization mechanism powered by the Open Policy Agent (OPA).

*   **Sanitization Policies:** Before an artifact is packaged, it is evaluated against OPA `.rego` policies. These policies redact sensitive fields, headers, and values based on your configuration.
*   **Hard Gates:** An artifact will *not* be exported if it fails the OPA policy checks. The final artifact manifest explicitly logs the `policyVersion` used and the number of `fieldsRedacted`.
*   **Reviewer Sign-off:** Certain environments may require cryptographic attestations (`reviewedBy`) before an artifact is finalized.

## 4. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

Because DAWG is a local-first tool:

*   **Storage:** Your artifacts are stored on your local file system as OCI-compliant tarballs/zstd archives.
*   **Security:** We strongly advise that you treat these artifacts as sensitive data, especially if captured from staging or production environments. Always review the artifact's contents locally before sharing it with third parties.
*   **Retention:** You are entirely responsible for the retention and deletion of your artifacts. We do not impose any automated deletion schedules on your local machine.

## 5. User Rights (Data Access and Deletion)

Under data protection laws (such as GDPR, CCPA, or the Indonesian PDP Law), you have rights regarding your personal data. 

Because we do not collect or store your data on our servers, you exercise these rights directly on your own machine. You can view, modify, or permanently delete any captured data simply by deleting the DAWG artifact files from your local storage.

## 6. Zero Telemetry & Analytics

Our software is built with a **Zero Telemetry** philosophy. We do not embed third-party trackers, we do not collect usage statistics, and we do not send automated crash reports. Your usage of DAWG is entirely private to you.

## 7. Your Responsibility as a Data Controller

If you use DAWG to capture data from your users or customers, you act as the **Data Controller** under applicable privacy laws. It is your responsibility to ensure that any data you capture, sanitize, and distribute using DAWG complies with your organization's legal obligations and privacy notices.

## 8. Contact & Support

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or encounter potential vulnerabilities in our OPA sanitization engine, please open an issue in our project repository.
